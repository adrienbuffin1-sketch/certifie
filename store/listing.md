**Description:**
Certifié is a daily trainer for French speakers preparing for Australia's Certified Translator test, French into English.

Every part of the app targets the places candidates lose marks.

WORDS THAT COST MARKS
• 650+ cards: false friends (actuellement, éventuellement, important…), civil status, migration, education, work, tax, housing, building, insurance, legal, police, health, government, contracts, manuals and business letters
• Type the English, not just flip: answers are checked, small typos forgiven
• Every word comes with a French example sentence and its English translation, plus audio
• Miss a word and you write it correctly once to lock it in
• Spaced repetition brings each word back just before you'd forget it
• Weak-words deck and a multiple-choice mode for quick sessions

TEST TRACK: THE CERTIFIED TRANSLATOR TEST, SIMULATED
• Full mock test: choose 2 of 3 general texts (news, government information, brochures, opinion pieces, notices), then a revision task, in 3.5 hours, like the real test
• Each task comes with a brief table: domain, topic, text type, purpose, audience
• Plain editor with word count and timer; spellcheck optional, autocorrect off
• Revision drills: correct 14–18 seeded errors and categorise each one with NAATI's error categories; unnecessary changes count against you
• Marking estimate against the CT rubric criteria (meaning transfer, textual norms and conventions, language proficiency, revision skills, error categorisation), with every error explained and a model translation
• Personal error log: your top 5 recurring error types feed new cards and drills
• Drill banks: false friends, small words that carry meaning, nouns to verbs, register
• Progress: estimates over time, time per text, errors per 250 words and a readiness indicator

GAMES THAT LEVEL UP
• Speed: four answers, 8 seconds a word, stars for speed
• Pairs: match French and English against the clock
• In context: fill the gap in a real French sentence
• True or false: 60-second blitz with streak multipliers
• 68 sets per game: master one to unlock the next. Earn XP and climb seven ranks from Rookie to Master translator
• Every word you miss goes back into your reviews

GRAMMAR TRAPS
• 49 English errors French speakers make, as one-tap drills: depuis + present, faire faire, "the" with general ideas, "or" as a connector, contract "shall", dates, numbers and money formats
• Searchable rule list with the wrong and right version side by side

READ THE NEWS
• 16 French news stories across the areas the test draws on: health, courts, immigration, work, housing, banking, insurance, schools, environment, tech, transport, farming, tourism, social services, police and business
• Read a paragraph, translate it yourself, then reveal the English
• Save each story's key terms straight into your cards, with the sentence from the story as the example
• Live headlines from France 24 and RFI to translate, with links to the full article and the English edition

TIMED TRANSLATIONS
• 12 ten-minute texts (~60 words) for a daily drill: parcel notices, school notes, pharmacy labels, insurance claims, job ads, contracts
• 14 test-style texts from different domains: contracts, appliance manuals, business letters, government brochures, health advice, personal documents
• One-hour timer, word count, spellcheck off like in the test
• Compare your version with a model translation, read the traps to watch for, then mark yourself on meaning, conventions, English and accuracy

REVISION TASK TRAINER
• 8 weak translations to fix
• Spot-the-error mode: tap every mistake, see what you missed and what you flagged by mistake
• Rewrite mode to practise writing the corrections

STAY ON TRACK
• Today plan: 20 cards, 5 drills, one news story, a 10-minute translation
• Streaks, levels from Beginner to Certified level, a knowledge map of every deck and a countdown to your test date
• Optional daily reminder
• Test facts, a formats cheat sheet and a checklist of the steps to booking your test

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your phone, with a backup file you control.

Certifié is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NAATI. Practice texts are written for the app in the style of test passages. Always check naati.com.au for the current test format and fees.

## App Review notes
Certifié is an offline study app for French speakers preparing for the Australian Certified Translator test (French into English). No login is needed. All content is built in: vocabulary cards (Words tab), grammar drills (Grammar tab), timed practice passages and a tap-the-error revision trainer (Practice tab), and test information (Test tab). Settings (gear icon, top right) has an optional daily local reminder, backup/restore of progress through the share sheet, and reset. The app collects no data. "NAATI" is used only descriptively, to name the test the app helps users prepare for; the app states that it is independent and not affiliated with NAATI.
