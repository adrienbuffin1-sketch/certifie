Certifié

A daily trainer for French speakers preparing for Australia's Certified Translator test, French into English.

What's inside

650+ words that cost marks (false friends, admin, legal, health, contracts) with spaced repetition; 49 English grammar traps for French speakers as quick drills; 14 test-style passages with model answers and self-marking; 8 revision tasks with a tap-the-error trainer; a formats cheat sheet and a step-by-step path to the test.

Certifié is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by NAATI.

Privacy policy

Certifié does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Your progress (words studied, drafts, test date, settings) is saved only on your device. If you use Back up progress, the app creates a file and hands it to you through the iPhone share sheet; where it goes is your choice. If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your device by iOS.

When you open Live headlines in the Read tab, the app downloads the public news feeds of France 24 and RFI directly from those sites. Nothing about you is sent beyond the standard request any website receives.

Links to external websites (such as naati.com.au, France 24 and RFI) open in your browser and are covered by those sites' own policies.
