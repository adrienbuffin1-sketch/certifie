Allowed and not allowed

Allowed: paper and offline dictionaries, and the online resources on NAATI's approved list.

Not allowed: Google Translate, DeepL, AI tools, translation software, spellcheck.

The revision task

You get a French text and a weak English translation. Fix errors of meaning, grammar, conventions and register, and explain your changes. Don't rewrite for style: examiners are told to look for real errors, not preferences. Train it in Practice → Revision.
