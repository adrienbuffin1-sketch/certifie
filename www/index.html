How it is marked

Translation tasks: Meaning transfer (pass: Band 2 or above), Application of textual norms and conventions (Band 3 or above), Language proficiency enabling meaning transfer (Band 2 or above).

Revision task: Revision skills (Band 2 or above) and Error categorisation (Band 3 or above).

You must pass all three tasks. If you pass two, you can apply for a supplementary test of the one you failed.

Allowed and not allowed

Allowed: hard-copy or offline dictionaries, glossaries and thesauri; NAATI's whitelist of online resources; the browser's basic spellcheck in translation tasks; pen and paper.

Not allowed: machine translation, AI or translation tools, Microsoft Word, other grammar or spelling tools, a second screen.

English spelling

NAATI accepts Australian, British, American or other English, as long as you use one variety consistently. If you mix them, examiners assess against Australian English (Macquarie Dictionary, Australian Government Style Manual). Certifié's models use Australian English.

The revision task

You get a French text and an English translation with 14 to 18 errors of meaning transfer and language. Highlight each error, correct it and choose one category from NAATI's list (distortion, unjustified omission, unjustified addition, inappropriate register, unidiomatic expression, grammar/syntax, spelling, punctuation). Do not revise for style or personal preference. Train it above in Revision drill.
